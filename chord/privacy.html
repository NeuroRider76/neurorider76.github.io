Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Applies to the Chord iOS app and its backend ("Chord", "we").

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple identifier. A random ID Apple gives us so we know it is you. We do not receive your name or email unless you choose to share them.
  • Voice recordings. Sent to our server only to be transcribed. The audio is deleted right after transcription and is never stored.
  • Transcripts and generated text. Kept so you can see, edit and reuse your drafts and notes. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: transcribe what you said, turn it into a post or a note, and deliver it where you asked. No advertising, no profiling, no selling or sharing of your data.

Service providers

We use these processors to run the app, under their own data protection terms: Railway (hosting and database), Groq and OpenAI (speech-to-text), Anthropic (text formatting). They receive only what is needed for that step.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Chord uses that access only to create note files in the folder of your Obsidian vault you chose. It does not read, change or delete your other files, and never shares Drive data with anyone.

Chord's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Posting to X

Chord does not connect to your X account. It opens X's own compose screen with your text filled in; you decide whether to post.

Retention and deletion

Audio: deleted immediately after transcription. Transcripts, drafts and notes: kept until you delete them or ask us to. Email im.alessiocongia@gmail.com and we will delete your account data within 30 days. Notes already written to your Drive stay yours.

Security

Every request to our server is authenticated. Data travels over HTTPS. Access tokens are short-lived and stored in the iOS Keychain.

Children

Chord is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions: im.alessiocongia@gmail.com
